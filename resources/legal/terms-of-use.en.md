# Hector terms of use

*Provisional version, pending legal review. Translated from the French text, which prevails.*

## What Hector is

Hector is an assistant based on artificial intelligence. Its answers are produced automatically: they may be incomplete or wrong, even when they cite a source.

## What Hector does not do

- Hector does not replace an electrician, a measurement, or an inspection of the installation.
- Hector certifies nothing and never says that an installation is de-energised.
- Only work on an electrical installation if you are qualified to do so. If in any doubt, call a professional.
- In case of immediate danger (burning smell, smoke, sparks, electric shock), move away and call the emergency services: 18 or 112.

## Your data

- Your conversations are kept so that you can find them again.
- Without an account, your data is deleted after 30 days without using the application.
- You can delete your account and its data at any time from your profile.

## Free use

Use without an account is limited. Beyond that limit, you will need to create an account to continue.
